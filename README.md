# Ch Swetha & C Lokesh — South Indian Christian Wedding Invitation

A digital wedding invitation web app designed for **Ch Swetha & C Lokesh**, celebrating their Holy Matrimony & Reception.

## Wedding Details
- **Bride:** Ch Swetha
- **Groom:** C Lokesh
- **Ceremony Type:** South Indian Christian Wedding
- **Date:** Wednesday, October 21, 2026 (21-10-2026)
- **Venue:** Vishnu Mahal, Minjur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu

## Features Included
1. **Interactive Opening Envelope Experience:**
   - Wax seal / monogram ("S & L") and gold holy cross emblem
   - "Open Invitation" with falling rose petals shower
2. **Caricature Couple Integration:**
   - Custom watercolor caricature illustration created from reference photos (Groom waving "Hi!", Bride in gown and veil)
   - Interactive floating sticker at the bottom-left with conversational speech bubbles on click
   - Spotlight portrait card in the wedding overview
3. **Official Digital Invitation Poster:**
   - High-resolution poster with glowing cross, Bible scripture (Mark 10:9), elegant typography, and caricature couple
   - One-click "Download Poster (PNG)" for WhatsApp and print
   - One-click "Share on WhatsApp"
4. **Clean, Guest-Friendly (No Intrusive Form Inputs):**
   - No names, phone numbers, or member counts requested from guests
   - Direct 1-click **Add to Google Calendar**
   - Direct 1-click **Save to Phone Calendar (.ics)**
5. **Interactive Blessings & Love Counter:**
   - Tap "Send Love ❤️" button with animated floating hearts
   - Quick-select blessing chips
   - Public blessing wall
6. **Venue & Directions:**
   - Vishnu Mahal, Minjur, Chennai with Google Maps navigation link
7. **Audio:**
   - Soft instrumental background music with play/pause toggle

## How to Run Locally
Double-click `run.bat` or run:
```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your web browser.
